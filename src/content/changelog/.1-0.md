title: 'Sonaru ships: every release from every artist you follow, in order'
---

The first version does one thing, and the whole app is built around getting it right. If
an artist you follow puts out a record, it is in your feed - not ranked below something
more popular, not folded into a weekly digest, not dropped because it was a single rather
than an album.

### What is in it

- **The feed.** Every release from every artist you follow, newest first. A list rather
  than a ranking, so nothing is left out to make room for something the app would rather
  show you.
- **Following.** Search for an artist and follow them, or bring your library across and
  follow everyone already in it.
- **Notifications** on release day, per artist, within about an hour of a record landing.
  They are on the free side and they stay there.
- **Genres and recommendations**, so a feed that has grown past a few hundred artists can
  be read one part at a time.
- **Likes, playlists, and auto-add** - a release from an artist you follow lands in a
  playlist without you opening the app.
- **Following friends**, and sharing what you have found.

Sonaru plays music through the subscription you already have; it is not a streaming
service and does not want to be one. Pro adds multiple auto-add playlists with rules per
genre or artist, recommendations down to the album and the song, and advanced sharing -
the [pricing page](/pricing) has the full comparison.

### What is not in it yet

No Android app and no web player. Everything below the feed - the mirror that watches for
releases, the notification path, the playlist writes - runs server-side and is shared, so
those are a client away rather than a rebuild.

Nothing here recommends its way around the feed, either. Discovery and completeness are
different problems and this release is almost entirely the second one; there is
[a post about why](/blog/what-is-sonaru).
